# Trustworthy Payment-Risk Venture Blueprint

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Reach a decision-complete Buildathon Blueprint for one Razorpay AI Buildathon venture, with two evidence-scored runners-up, ready to hand off for implementation. The selected venture must have a credible startup path, a measurable MVP, a safe agent model, a cost-aware resource stack, a demo and evaluation contract, and an India-first expansion thesis.

## Notes

- Domain: agentic fintech, identity assurance, fraud defense, payment operations, and startup planning.
- Every session should consult Wayfinder; grilling and domain-modeling apply to founder decisions, research applies to external evidence, and prototype applies when concrete behavior or presentation needs human reaction.
- Optimize approximately 60% for Razorpay Buildathon signal and 40% for durable startup value.
- The leading hypothesis is an India-first Trust Agent for urgent payout instructions received through calls or voice messages. It assembles a Risk Case and submits it to a deterministic Policy Gate before a Money Action.
- Evidence may overturn the leading hypothesis. Selection must compare it transparently with two runners-up.
- SIH26104 contributes real-time voice-clone detection; SIH26168 contributes degraded-signal reasoning, sensor fusion, and graceful recovery; SIH26188 contributes fake-identity and document screening. They are market signals and design inspiration, not mandatory feature requirements.
- Solo founder; development will be agent-driven, with each day until submission available. Scope must remain independently reproducible and operable.
- No direct customer access is expected before submission. Use the founder's Gemini Deep Research summaries, then reconcile consequential claims against primary or otherwise high-trust sources.
- Prefer free, open, local, test-mode, or credit-backed resources whenever capability is comparable. Paid components are acceptable when their incremental value is explicit and no credible free alternative exists.
- India-first, globally extensible. Sensitive Money Actions remain bounded, gated, explainable, logged, and human-controlled.

## Out of scope

- Building the product, training production models, creating the public repository, recording the pitch, or submitting the application; this map ends at a decision-complete implementation handoff.
- Offensive fraud tooling, real-money autonomous execution, covert surveillance, or bypassing identity/payment controls.
- Forcing all three SIH inspirations into the selected MVP when evidence does not justify them.
- A formal legal opinion, production regulatory certification, carrier-level telephony deployment, or specialized navigation hardware.
- Pretending synthetic evidence is customer validation; limitations must remain visible in the Blueprint.
