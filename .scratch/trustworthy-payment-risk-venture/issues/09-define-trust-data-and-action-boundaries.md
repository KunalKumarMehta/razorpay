# Define Trust, Data, and Action Boundaries

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 07

## Question

What may each agent observe, infer, retain, recommend, and execute; what must the deterministic Policy Gate validate; when is human approval mandatory; how are consent, PII, uncertainty, overrides, stopping rules, failure recovery, and the audit trail represented?

## Answer

**Authority and action.** PayoutProof has an absolute three-part separation: the Trust Agent observes permitted evidence, derives provenance-linked findings, and recommends a response; the deterministic Policy Gate alone evaluates a frozen case snapshot; and a human alone initiates handoff into the existing approval rail. The rail retains maker-checker approval, execution, ledger, and settlement authority. No agent, model score, administrator, exception, or emergency can override the gate. An eligible handoff uses an immutable, expiring, single-use **Handoff Grant** bound to the tenant, case, exact Payment Intent and Risk Case snapshot hashes, policy version, outcome, and nonce. The adapter verifies the grant, fresh human gesture, actor, unchanged intent, expiry, and idempotency; it cannot mutate the intent, and neither agent nor gate receives payout credentials.

**Observation and inference.** The MVP observes only an operator-submitted request bundle and case-scoped, allowlisted, least-privilege read-only organizational records. It excludes ambient monitoring, broad inbox or drive crawling, covert collection, contact discovery, and outbound impersonation checks. Raw evidence is compartmentalized by function rather than shared across agent context; the Policy Gate sees only structured data and the action adapter sees only minimum Payment Intent fields. Permitted inference is limited to enumerated intent facts, evidence quality and consistency, destination status, and signal-level risk indicators. Identity-from-voice, guilt, intent, emotion, protected attributes, generalized trust scores, and unrelated profiling are prohibited.

**Truth, data, and processing authority.** Findings preserve source, method, version, quality, and one of **supported**, **contradicted**, **not observed**, **insufficient quality**, or **not evaluated**; there is no universal fraud probability, and missing, failed, stale, or low-quality evidence is never safe. The Buildathon uses local processing and synthetic or explicitly redacted evidence, with speaker enrollment and real KYC documents disabled. Each admitted item carries a **Processing Authority Record** covering its data class, source, subject category, submitter, purpose, asserted authority or consent reference, permitted uses, processing route, redaction, retention, legal hold, and restrictions; incomplete required authority blocks admission. Future hosted processors are default-off and require tenant-admin enablement, reviewed processing terms, region and subprocessor disclosure, encryption, bounded retention, deletion support, and no-training commitments. Raw evidence, PII, transcripts, and secrets never enter free-tier AI, telemetry, crash, support, or observability systems.

**Retention and audit.** Raw media is transient and deleted after extraction by default; optional review retention is explicit, short, and visibly expiring. Only necessary redacted excerpts persist. Structured Risk Cases and audit events follow tenant-configured schedules, with legal holds and deletion tombstones recorded as events; the synthetic demo exposes configuration and reset without asserting a universal production period. Every material evidence, model, human, policy, retention, and adapter transition enters a tamper-evident append-only event chain with ordering, timestamps, actors, versions, outcomes, reason codes, hashes, and correlation or idempotency identifiers. Audit storage contains redacted values and hashes, not raw evidence.

**Policy Gate and outcomes.** The gate validates tenant and actor authority, schema, case state, canonical intent and evidence integrity, provenance, required evidence, quality and freshness, contradictions, destination status, financial limits, separation of duties, duplicate state, active policy version, and material changes. It never fetches data, invokes a model, repairs input, or interprets free text. Its only **Policy Outcomes** are **BLOCKED**, **HOLD**, **STEP_UP_REQUIRED**, and **ELIGIBLE_FOR_HANDOFF**, returned with stable reasons, required next steps, evaluated intent hash, policy version, timestamp, and expiry. These are workflow states, not fraud verdicts, and change only through reevaluation.

**Human control, policy change, and recovery.** Humans freshly confirm extracted Payment Intent fields and separately initiate eligible handoff. The Payment Operator may correct evidence or request reevaluation; the Finance Control Owner may record an exception disposition; neither can manufacture eligibility. Policies are immutable and versioned, regression-tested, separately approved before prospective activation, and never retroactively rewrite an outcome. Processing fails closed under explicit time, retry, and attempt budgets: remediable missing evidence requires step-up; contradictions, dependency failure, expiry, or uncertain downstream state cause a hold; unauthorized, tampered, prohibited, or malformed input is blocked. Repeated unresolved states and material corrections stop autonomy. Ambiguous handoff status is reconciled by idempotency state and never blindly retried.
