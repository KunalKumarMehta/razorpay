# Define the Value Proof and Evaluation Contract

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 03, 07

## Question

What falsifiable claims must the MVP prove, on what representative corpus or simulator, using which success, safety, latency, false-positive-cost, exception, and money-impact metrics, with what held-out tests and honest limitations?

## Answer

**Claim.** PayoutProof must prove workflow-level decision support: on a disclosed held-out capability corpus, it binds supplied evidence to the exact Payment Intent, returns the policy-correct next action, produces zero Unsafe Handoffs in the Critical Safety Suite, and reduces simulated manual evidence choreography. Component metrics are diagnostic. The evaluation does not prove production fraud detection, identity, beneficiary ownership, customer ROI, willingness to pay, or realized loss prevention.

**Gold contract.** Each Evaluation Case is generated from structured simulator truth and scored against an independent frozen policy oracle. The canonical Policy Outcomes are `BLOCKED`, `HOLD`, `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`, and `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HANDOFF`. The headline Workflow Evaluation Corpus uses the latter three, with gold output comprising the exact Payment Intent plus its outcome. `BLOCKED` is reserved for structurally invalid, prohibited, tampered, or unauthorized cases in the Critical Safety Suite and policy diagnostics; it is scored as a required protective non-handoff and four-outcome correctness, but excluded from headline precision/recall. Protective Intervention Required in the headline corpus means gold `HOLD` or `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`; any non-handoff prediction is a predicted positive. An Unsafe Handoff is an eligible result despite a gold protective outcome, missing required evidence, an incorrect/unresolved/unsupported intent, a material post-evaluation edit, or an unsafe fallback. Intent Binding Correct requires an all-or-nothing match on counterparty, destination, amount/currency, purpose, instruction reference, and provenance; ambiguity must remain unresolved, and eligible handoff requires a complete immutable intent hash.

**Suites and corpus.** Keep three suites separate:

- a Workflow Evaluation Corpus with 45 development and 90 sealed held-out cases, including 30 each for `HOLD`, `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`, and `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HANDOFF`, 30 per English/Hindi/English-Hindi code-switching stratum, and 60% raw-audio cases;
- 27 separate Critical Safety Cases, three across each frozen invariant category: material-intent error, unapproved/changed destination, missing/contradictory evidence, unusable audio/model failure, spoof/replay with authority cues, extraction/schema failure, material mutation, duplicate/replay, and policy failure or evidence-borne bypass attempt; and
- independent Component Stress Sets: 90 ASR clips, 120 anti-spoof clips (at least 60 bona fide/60 spoof), 90 extraction bundles, and, if implemented, 60 OCR captures.

The workflow corpus uses published pairwise capability coverage across action, destination state, material fields, evidence completeness/quality, voice/text/optional-document modality, language/channel, business context, edits, and independently seeded legitimate/malicious/indeterminate status. It is not a prevalence sample. Voice notes are primary; text/messages are required; documents remain optional diagnostics. Language claims are limited to evaluated demo coverage, reported per stratum, with fail-closed behavior on unusable transcription.

**Provenance and holdout.** Generate fictitious business facts and labels from the versioned simulator. Media may use disclosed TTS/synthetic sources, explicit-consent recordings, or legally usable public slices mainly for diagnostics; record source, consent/license basis, version, seed, transformations, and hash, with no real credentials or customer PII. Separate development and held-out data across scenario templates, wording, speakers/voices, media sources, and degradation recipes. Freeze manifests, normalization, prompts, models, settings, and thresholds before the sealed run. Keep simulator truth, independent declarative oracle, runtime Policy Gate, and scorer separate; validate invariants automatically and manually audit every safety case plus a stratified 30-case workflow sample before sealing. Any test-informed change contaminates that evaluation version and requires a new corpus.

**Acceptance gates.** Hard gates are zero Unsafe Handoffs across all 81 repeated Critical Safety executions; zero eligible handoffs with an incorrect, unresolved, unsupported, or mutated material intent; and fail-closed behavior for every schema failure, timeout, missing policy, or unavailable required signal. Held-out gates are Protective Intervention Required recall at least 95%, precision at least 85%, exact three-action correctness at least 90%, Intent Binding Correct at least 95%, correct abstention at least 95%, and no language stratum below 90% intervention recall or 90% Intent Binding Correct. Report headline three-action and separately scoped four-outcome safety/policy confusion matrices and correctness, plus integer counts, denominators, Wilson intervals, strata, and uncovered cells; misses remain visible and narrow the claim.

**Stability, latency, and diagnostics.** Use one sealed headline run, repeat all safety cases three times, and repeat a stratified 20-case workflow subset five times; report action/intent consistency, schema failures, and latency spread, and fail the safety gate if any repeated safety run yields an Unsafe Handoff. On the named reference machine, require warm text p95 at most five seconds, warm voice p95 at most 60 seconds for recordings up to 30 seconds, and cold voice p95 at most 120 seconds, with median/p95/maximum, real-time factor, cold/warm, stage, stratum, failure, and longer-recording results. Publish only implemented component diagnostics: ASR WER/CER and entity accuracy; anti-spoof ROC-AUC/EER and frozen-threshold false accepts/rejects; extraction schema validity, exact fields, unsupported fields, and abstention; optional OCR field/quality/abstention; and deterministic policy coverage. Never aggregate them into product accuracy or claim domain transfer.

**Workflow value, false positives, and money.** Compare identical cases against a predeclared no-PayoutProof workflow using the same evidence, context, and policy. Require at least 30% fewer operator interactions or process steps without correctness degradation. A 25% median paired active-time reduction is a gate only with at least three randomized, blinded independent reviewers; founder-only timing is descriptive. Report unnecessary hold and step-up separately as counts, interactions, minutes, and modeled delay-cost ranges. Report simulated payout exposure safely routed, modeled handling-cost change under disclosed low/base/high assumptions, and false-positive burden separately, including counts, distributions, median, maximum, sensitivity, and the result with the largest case removed. Never label these realized savings, prevented loss, or ROI.

**Exceptions.** An exception must preserve partial intent, identify unresolved fields, emit a frozen reason code, request specific evidence/action, and remain non-handoff. Report identification recall, false exceptions, reason-code and exact-next-step accuracy, unhandled exceptions, and safe-exception rate. Designate 18 held-out step-up cases, six per language, for separate lifecycle testing with sufficient unchanged evidence, irrelevant/insufficient evidence, and a material intent edit. Score against independent frozen post-evidence actions; unresolved cases remain stepped up, material edits invalidate evaluation, and any unsafe transition is an Unsafe Handoff. Do not add these follow-up runs to headline precision/recall.

**Failure accounting, reproducibility, and limitations.** Score every sealed case. Crashes, invalid schemas, missing outputs, timeouts, and media failures are incorrect even when fail-closed; only prehashed spares may replace pre-result packaging corruption. Preserve and label every rerun. Publish the simulator, oracle, manifests, normalization, seeds, hashes, provenance/licenses, frozen configuration, scorer, hardware/environment, append-only raw outputs, and documented regeneration/scoring commands; represent secrets and nonredistributable media with hashes, acquisition instructions, or synthetic substitutes. Every result must state synthetic-design and unknown-prevalence limits, small-sample uncertainty, domain shift, narrow language coverage, absence of customer/operator validation and live-money/approval-workflow testing, lack of identity/authenticity/ownership/fraud proof, machine-specific latency, licensing constraints, and reviewer bias. A hard-gate miss means the build is a working prototype that did not satisfy this contract.
