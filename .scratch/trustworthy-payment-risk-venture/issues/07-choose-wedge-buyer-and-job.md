# Choose the Startup Wedge, Economic Buyer, and Job to Be Done

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02, 04, 06

## Question

For the selected venture, what exact risky event begins the workflow, who experiences it, who pays, what job are they hiring the product to perform, what existing behavior is displaced, and where does the first product boundary stop?

## Answer

**Wedge and first customer.** PayoutProof first serves corporate finance and treasury teams at workflow-qualified growth and mid-market Indian businesses already using an existing payout rail. The initial customer has centralized finance or treasury, recurring vendor or partner payouts, maker-checker approval, out-of-band exceptions, and no dedicated fraud-investigation function. The segment remains cross-sector until customer discovery supports a vertical or numeric size band.

**Trigger.** A Risk Case opens when a trusted-seeming, out-of-band instruction asks finance to make a time-sensitive payout for a known business relationship to a bank account, VPA, or equivalent destination not already approved for that counterparty. It opens when the instruction is received, before a candidate payout is created. Amount thresholds and the lifecycle of an Approved Destination remain policy decisions.

**User and buyer.** The primary user is the **Payment Operator**: the payment maker, AP analyst, treasury analyst, or finance-operations equivalent who receives the instruction and prepares the payout. The economic buyer is the **Finance Control Owner**, usually the Controller or Head of Finance, who owns payout-process integrity, exception handling, and maker-checker effectiveness; the CFO or Treasurer is the executive sponsor. The checker or approver is the principal downstream consumer of the Risk Case.

**Job to be done.**

> When a trusted-seeming, time-sensitive instruction asks me to pay a known counterparty at a destination we have not approved, help me preserve and reconcile the scattered request evidence into an exact, reviewable Payment Intent and required safe next step before I create or submit the payout, so I can move legitimate exceptions quickly without letting familiarity or urgency substitute for verification.

The Finance Control Owner hires PayoutProof to make exception handling faster, consistent, and auditable while keeping maker-checker authority intact.

**Behavior displaced.** PayoutProof replaces manual evidence choreography: ad hoc transcription, searching prior beneficiary and payment history, comparing messages or invoices, inconsistently collecting callback evidence, and forwarding screenshots or narratives through chat or email. These current-state details are customer-discovery hypotheses, not validated universal facts. Independent callback or step-up verification, finance policy, maker-checker approval, and the existing payout rail remain in place.

**First product boundary.** PayoutProof ingests the operator-supplied request bundle and read-only business context, extracts the exact proposed Payment Intent, reconciles evidence, preserves provenance and uncertainty in a Risk Case, and submits that intent to a deterministic Policy Gate. The gate returns **hold**, **obtain specified step-up evidence**, or **eligible for human handoff**. For an eligible case, the Payment Operator explicitly initiates creation of the exact, unchanged intent as a pending item in the existing approval rail. PayoutProof attaches the Risk Case and policy outcome; the rail owns maker-checker approval, execution, ledger state, and settlement. A material edit invalidates the evaluation and requires a new one. Buildathon handoff is test-mode or simulated.

**Explicit non-claims.** PayoutProof is not a fraud oracle, standalone deepfake detector, beneficiary-ownership guarantee, generic KYC or fraud platform, replacement approval dashboard, or autonomous payment agent. Buyer willingness to pay, the detailed current-state workflow, and the best vertical remain unvalidated and require customer discovery.
