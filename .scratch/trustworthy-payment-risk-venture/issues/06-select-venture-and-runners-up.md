# Select the Venture and Two Runners-Up

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05

## Question

Which venture should become the Buildathon submission, and which two should remain documented runners-up, when scored on Buildathon fit, measurable proof, severity of customer pain, market potential, differentiation, agentic depth, solo feasibility, resource availability, defensibility, regulatory exposure, and future expansion?

## Answer

The founder selected the recommended podium in the [Venture Selection Scorecard](../research/06-venture-selection-scorecard.md):

1. **Winner — PayoutProof: Urgent Payout Trust Agent** (**88.2/100**). It best combines the AI Risk Manager's measurable defense-only contract, a strong five-minute agentic demo, local/test-mode solo feasibility, and differentiated request-to-authorization evidence binding behind a deterministic Policy Gate and human approval.
2. **Runner-up #1 — ChangeGuard: Beneficiary-Change Verification Agent** (**71.4/100**). It is the simpler, highly measurable fallback, but has weaker agentic depth and defensibility in a crowded document and accounts-payable control space.
3. **Runner-up #2 — RecallReady: Payment-Fraud Recovery Triage Agent** (**67.4/100**). It addresses severe, time-sensitive pain and has a credible coordination expansion path, but cannot honestly prove recovered money, institutional access, or external execution within the Buildathon window.

The scores are comparative decision aids, not validated market measurements. India-specific impersonation prevalence and buyer willingness to pay remain unvalidated; audio-model performance may degrade on real Indian phone channels; and public evidence cannot rule out equivalent private incumbent workflows. These uncertainties constrain the claims but do not overturn PayoutProof's lead for this Buildathon Blueprint.
