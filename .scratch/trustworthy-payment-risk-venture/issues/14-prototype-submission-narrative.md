# Prototype the Submission Narrative and Five-Minute Pitch

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 06, 08, 10, 13

## Question

Which problem-to-proof narrative, architecture view, live demo beats, failure story, measured results, startup thesis, repository evidence, and final ask should fit into the required submission artifacts and five-minute pitch without overstating validation?

## Answer

The resolved narrative is embodied in the self-contained [PayoutProof five-minute pitch rehearsal](../prototypes/payoutproof-pitch-rehearsal-throwaway.html). Its pure state model, free-play controls, and guided pass, miss, not-run, claim-drift, and timing-pressure walkthroughs enforce one 4:45 sequence, one Evaluation Version, honest result wording, cross-artifact agreement, and fail-closed release rules.

**Proof thesis and opening.** Ask judges to believe one narrow claim: PayoutProof turns scattered urgent-payout evidence into an exact, auditable Payment Intent and a policy-correct safe next step before the existing approval rail; a disclosed sealed synthetic evaluation tests required intervention and zero Unsafe Handoff. Voice/deepfake analysis is a non-authoritative input, productivity and architecture are supporting proof, and threat is motivating context only. Open for 28 seconds on one clearly labelled synthetic voice-note/message case requesting ₹4.25 lakh to an unapproved changed account. Frame the Payment Operator's need to determine what is supported, missing, and safe to enter the rail. Do not assert that the speaker is fake, the request fraudulent, or prevalence/customer pain validated.

**Architecture and demo.** Use one three-lane authority diagram around a frozen Payment Intent: permitted evidence and a bounded local Trust Agent produce provenance-linked findings; the deterministic Policy Gate alone returns `BLOCKED`, `HOLD`, `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`, or `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HANDOFF`; a fresh human gesture and single-use exact-intent Handoff Grant may create a pending item in the existing maker-checker rail, which retains approval and execution. Models cannot set policy or access payout credentials. Place the independent simulator/oracle/scorer verification spine below; keep component/model detail in the README.

The 85-second primary demo begins after admission and extraction and uses exactly three meaningful human gestures. The operator confirms every material field and freezes the intent hash; policy requires independent callback plus separate destination approval; callback alone remains `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`; separately governed destination approval earns `ELIGIBLE_FOR_HANDOFF`; fresh human initiation consumes the grant and creates exactly one pending item in the fake existing rail. Stop before approval or execution. The 30-second graceful failure forks the same valid handoff into unknown adapter status: preserve historical eligibility, enter `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, make the grant unavailable, press retry once, and show deterministic rejection with no second pending item. Narrate: “Uncertainty never becomes permission.” Material-edit invalidation remains a repository-tested invariant rather than a second live failure.

**Results and repository proof.** Populate the pitch only from an immutable sealed result manifest using explicit `NOT RUN`, `PASSED`, or `MISSED` state; never render a target as a result. Present, in order: Unsafe Handoffs as an integer count over 81 repeated Critical Safety executions and related invariants; held-out intervention precision/recall, exact-action correctness, Intent Binding Correct, abstention, denominators, and per-language minima over 90 sealed cases; paired operator-interaction change labelled simulated and only when correctness is preserved; then latency and component diagnostics, never “product accuracy.” A hard-gate miss must be shown as “working prototype; contract not satisfied,” with the disabled unsafe route. Always disclose the synthetic designed corpus, unknown prevalence/domain shift, and absence of customer, live-money, identity, ownership, fraud, or ROI validation.

The public repository/release manifest is authoritative; the video explains and demonstrates; the form is a concise reconciled index; no separate deck is required. The README begins with one setup/demo command and a Claims → Evidence table linking every claim through the immutable release manifest to the authority architecture, schemas/lifecycle, independent simulator/oracle/scorer, sealed configurations and hashes, append-only raw and scored outputs, Critical Safety results, paired baseline, audit verification, fake-adapter/replay tests, source/license manifest, failures and disabled routes, limitations, and clean-clone commands. Show that table plus one terminal verification result and the repository URL/QR. Missing evidence is `NOT AVAILABLE`; clean-clone reproducibility is claimed only after it passes. Keep weights and nonredistributable media out of git with hashes, acquisition instructions, or synthetic substitutes.

**Startup thesis and ask.** Position PayoutProof as the urgent-payout-exception control layer for Finance Control Owners at workflow-qualified Indian growth and mid-market businesses. The next proof is three bounded Design Partners testing zero Unsafe Handoffs and at least 30% fewer operator interactions without correctness loss; expand into beneficiary-change controls only if at least two of three pass and commit. Models are replaceable; customer-specific control contracts, reviewer history, reproducible safety evidence, and rail integrations compound. Omit pricing, TAM, forecasts, prevented loss, and ROI from the video; retain the ₹1–2 lakh pilot and ₹6–12 lakh ARR ranges only in the repository as unvalidated hypotheses.

Ask judges to evaluate PayoutProof for AI Risk Manager on the disclosed workflow contract, sealed results, safe failure, and reproducible repository—not as a fraud oracle. If shortlisted, seek panel stress-testing and, aspirationally, introductions to Finance Control Owners for three bounded Design Partner validations without implying any Razorpay commitment, partnership, deployment, or API support. Hold a final frame with project/track, proof thesis, repository URL/QR, Evaluation Version plus honest status, and “No autonomous payout approval or execution.”

**Artifact choreography.** Produce a deterministic edited 4:45 video: 0:00–0:28 problem; 0:28–0:55 architecture; 0:55–2:20 main demo; 2:20–2:50 safe failure; 2:50–3:35 results and limitations; 3:35–3:55 repository evidence; 3:55–4:25 startup thesis; 4:25–4:45 final ask. The remaining 15 seconds protect against encoding/player variance. Cuts may remove waiting but must never splice code/result versions or imply one uninterrupted run. Show the Evaluation Version on every demo/result segment. Any conflict among the form, spoken/video claims, Claims → Evidence rows, URLs, result status, or limitations blocks submission.
